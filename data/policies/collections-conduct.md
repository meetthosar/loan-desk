# Recovery and field officer conduct

Udaan follows the RBI fair practices code for microfinance lenders. Every borrower can expect the following.

## When staff may contact you
Field officers and recovery staff may call or visit only between 08:00 and 19:00. Visits happen at the borrower's home or the group meeting place, never at her workplace without her consent.

## How staff must behave
Staff must carry an Udaan photo identity card and show it on request. They may not use abusive language, threats, intimidation or physical force, contact neighbours, relatives or the borrower's employer about the loan, or shame a borrower in public.

## Group meetings
Recovery is discussed only at the borrower's own group meeting or privately. No group member may be pressured to pay another member's EMI beyond what the joint liability agreement says.

## Reporting misconduct
Any borrower can report misconduct through the chat assistant, the support number or the branch. Reports about field officer behaviour are handled as high priority and passed to the Grievance Redressal Officer the same day. The borrower will not face any action for complaining.

## Hardship
A borrower facing a medical emergency, a death in the family, a natural disaster or a lost livelihood should tell the branch. The branch must consider her situation before any recovery visit.
