# Complaints and grievance redressal

## Level 1: Support desk
Complaints can be made through the chat assistant, the toll-free support number 1800-000-0000 (synthetic), email to support@udaan.example, or at any branch. Every complaint gets a ticket number. Udaan replies within 2 working days, or within 1 working day for high-priority complaints such as staff misconduct, and aims to resolve it within 7 working days.

## Level 2: Grievance Redressal Officer
If the complaint is not resolved within 7 working days, or the borrower is not satisfied, she can escalate to the Grievance Redressal Officer at grievance@udaan.example or by letter to the head office in Pune. The officer replies within 15 days. The officer's name and number are displayed at every branch.

## Level 3: RBI Ombudsman
If the complaint is not resolved within 30 days of the first complaint, or the borrower is not satisfied with the reply, she can complain to the RBI under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme at cms.rbi.org.in or by calling 14448. This service is free.

## Industry body
Udaan is a member of a self-regulatory organisation for microfinance lenders, which also accepts complaints about member lenders.

## What to include
The loan number, what happened and when, and what the borrower would like Udaan to do. Never include Aadhaar, PAN or bank passwords in a complaint.
