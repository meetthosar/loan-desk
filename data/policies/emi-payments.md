# Paying your EMI

## Ways to pay
- Auto-debit (NACH) from the bank account registered at disbursement, on the due date.
- UPI to the loan's payment address, shown in the Key Fact Statement and on every EMI reminder SMS.
- Cash to the field officer at the monthly group meeting, against a printed receipt.
- Cash or UPI at any Udaan branch, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 17:00.

## Reminders
Udaan sends an SMS reminder 3 days before each due date and on the due date itself.

## Receipts
Every payment is confirmed by SMS within 24 hours. A cash payment is valid only against a printed receipt; never pay a field officer without one.

## Pending payments
A UPI payment can show as pending for up to 48 hours. Do not pay again while a payment is pending: if it fails, the money is returned to the payer's bank account within 5 working days and the EMI can then be paid again.

## Paying before the due date
An EMI can be paid any time before its due date. It is applied on the due date.

## Checking your next due date
The next due date and EMI amount are in the Key Fact Statement, on the SMS reminder, and available from the chat assistant.
