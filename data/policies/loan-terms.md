# Loan terms

Udaan Microfinance gives joint liability group loans to women in low-income households. This page is a synthetic policy for a fictional lender, modelled on the RBI Master Direction on microfinance loans (2022). It is not a real lender's terms.

## Who can borrow
A borrower must be a member of an Udaan joint liability group of 5 to 10 women, with a household income of up to ₹3,00,000 a year. Total repayments on all microfinance loans of the household may not exceed 50% of monthly household income.

## Loan amount and tenure
Loans range from ₹15,000 to ₹60,000. Tenure is 12, 18 or 24 months. The loan amount, tenure, interest rate and total cost are shown in the Key Fact Statement signed before disbursement.

## EMI schedule
EMIs are monthly and of equal amount. The due date is the same day of the month as the disbursement date. If the loan was disbursed on the 29th, 30th or 31st, the due date is the 28th. The first EMI falls one month after disbursement.

## Key Fact Statement
Every borrower receives a Key Fact Statement in Hindi, Marathi or English before signing. It lists the annual interest rate, all charges, the repayment schedule and the grievance contact. Udaan may not charge any fee that is not in the Key Fact Statement.

## No collateral
Microfinance loans are unsecured. Udaan never asks for collateral, a security deposit or a guarantor outside the group.
