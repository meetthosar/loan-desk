# Privacy and your data

Udaan processes personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## What we collect
Name, phone number, address, identity documents collected at onboarding, household income, loan and repayment records, and the messages you send to support.

## Why we use it
To give and service the loan, to meet RBI and credit bureau reporting rules, to prevent fraud, and to answer your questions. We do not sell personal data or use it for marketing by other companies.

## Chat messages
Before a chat message is processed by the assistant, phone numbers, emails, Aadhaar, PAN and bank account numbers are removed or replaced. Chat transcripts are kept for 1 year to handle complaints, then deleted.

## Your rights
You can ask for a summary of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and withdraw consent for any use that is not required by law. Requests go to the Data Protection Officer at privacy@udaan.example and are answered within 30 days.

## Sharing
Repayment data is shared with credit bureaus as required by RBI. Data is shared with service providers (payments, SMS, support software) only to provide the service, under contract.
