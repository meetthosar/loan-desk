# Closing a loan early (foreclosure)

## Who can foreclose
Any borrower may close her loan early at any time. There is no lock-in period and no foreclosure charge.

## How the foreclosure amount is calculated
The foreclosure amount is:
- the outstanding principal,
- plus interest accrued since the last EMI that was paid, at the loan's annual rate, for each day up to the foreclosure date,
- plus penal charges on any overdue amount.

There is no prepayment charge. The chat assistant can give an exact quote for any date in the next 30 days.

## How long a quote is valid
A foreclosure quote is valid for 3 days from the foreclosure date it was made for. If payment is made later, interest for each extra day is added; the quote shows this per-day interest.

## How to pay
Pay the full foreclosure amount in one payment, by UPI to the loan's payment address or at a branch. Cash payments above ₹20,000 must be made at a branch, not to a field officer.

## After closure
Udaan sends a closure confirmation by SMS within 2 working days, cancels the NACH mandate within 7 working days, and issues a No Objection Certificate within 7 working days. The credit bureaus are updated in the next monthly report.

## Part prepayment
A borrower may also prepay part of the loan. The EMI stays the same and the tenure becomes shorter. There is no charge for part prepayment.
