# Fees and charges

This is the full list of charges on an Udaan microfinance loan. No other charge may be applied.

## Processing fee
A processing fee of 1% of the loan amount plus GST is deducted at disbursement. It is not refunded if the loan is closed early.

## Penal charges for late payment
If an EMI is not paid by its due date, a penal charge of 24% a year applies to the overdue amount only, for the number of days it stays overdue. Penal charges are simple, never compounded, and are not added to the interest rate. There is no penal charge on the part of the loan that is not yet due.

## Prepayment and foreclosure charge
There is no prepayment charge and no foreclosure charge. A borrower may repay part or all of the loan at any time without a fee.

## Payment bounce charge
There is no charge when an auto-debit (NACH) or UPI payment fails. Only the penal charge above applies, and only once the EMI is overdue.

## Duplicate documents
The first statement of account and the first No Objection Certificate are free. A duplicate copy costs ₹50 plus GST.

## Changes to charges
Udaan gives 30 days' written notice before any change to charges, and changes never apply to loans already disbursed.

## Waivers
Support staff and the chat assistant cannot waive or reduce any charge. A borrower may request a review in writing through a ticket; the branch manager decides within 15 working days.
