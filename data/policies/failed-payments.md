# Failed payments

## Why an auto-debit fails
The common reasons are not enough balance in the bank account on the due date, an expired or cancelled NACH mandate, or a frozen or dormant bank account.

## What happens after a failure
Udaan does not retry a failed auto-debit automatically. There is no bounce charge. The EMI becomes overdue from the due date, and a penal charge of 24% a year applies to the overdue amount until it is paid.

## How to pay after a failure
Pay the EMI by UPI or at a branch as soon as possible, ideally within 3 days. Paying soon keeps the penal charge small and keeps the record clean.

## Money debited but EMI shows unpaid
If the bank account was debited but the EMI still shows unpaid, do not pay again. Raise a ticket with the date and amount of the debit. Udaan reconciles it with the bank within 5 working days and either applies the payment or confirms that the bank will return the money.

## Expired mandate
If the NACH mandate has expired, a new mandate can be signed at the branch or with the field officer. Until then, pay by UPI or at a branch.

## Credit bureau reporting
Udaan reports every loan's repayment status to the credit bureaus every month. An EMI that is more than 30 days overdue on the reporting date is shown as late, which can make future loans harder to get.

## If you cannot pay
If a borrower expects to have trouble paying, she should tell the field officer or the branch early. The branch can explain the options; the chat assistant cannot change a repayment schedule.
