# Updating contact details

## Phone number and email
A borrower can change her registered mobile number or email in the chat. Udaan sends a one-time password (OTP) to the currently registered mobile number; the change is made once she enters the correct code. The OTP is valid for 10 minutes, and three wrong attempts cancel it.

## Lost access to the registered number
If the borrower no longer has the registered mobile number, she must visit her branch with an original identity document. The branch updates the number after checking her identity.

## Address and name changes
Address and name changes can only be made at a branch, with a supporting document.

## Keep your OTP private
Udaan staff will never ask for an OTP over a phone call or in person. Share the OTP only in the chat where you asked for the change.

## Aadhaar and other identity numbers
Udaan never asks for Aadhaar, PAN or bank account numbers in chat, SMS or email. If you share one by mistake, the assistant removes it and does not store it.
