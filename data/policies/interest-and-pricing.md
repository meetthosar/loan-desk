# Interest rate and pricing

## How interest is calculated
Interest is charged on a reducing balance: each month, interest is calculated only on the principal still outstanding. Interest accrues daily, using a 365-day year.

## Interest rate range
Annual interest rates are between 22% and 25%, depending on the loan cycle and the group's repayment record. The rate is fixed for the life of the loan and is shown in the Key Fact Statement.

## Pricing policy
Udaan's board approves a pricing policy that sets the rate range and the maximum spread over the cost of funds. The current range and the average rate charged last quarter are published on the website and at every branch.

## How an EMI is made up
Each EMI is the same amount. Early EMIs are mostly interest; later EMIs are mostly principal. The repayment schedule in the Key Fact Statement shows the split for every month.

## Paying early saves interest
Because interest is charged on the outstanding principal, paying part of the loan early reduces the interest on every later EMI. There is no charge for paying early.
